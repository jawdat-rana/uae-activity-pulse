Methodology and limitations

This analysis uses Dubai Land Department rent-registration exports collected weekly.

  • VERSION_EN = New
  • USAGE_EN = Commercial
  • PROP_TYPE_EN = Unit
  • TOTAL_PROPERTIES = 1

Virtual-unit and multi-property registrations are excluded. Because the public rent export does not expose a stable contract ID, rows that are identical across lease fields but differ only in project attribution are treated as one lease event. Area-level analysis is therefore more reliable than project-level attribution.

Four weeks is enough to produce a useful pulse, but not enough to label movements as seasonal or structural trends.
